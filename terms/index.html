Terms of Service

Deck District Stats is a private tool run by Deck District for its own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside Deck District can sign up or log in.

  • The tool only reads statistics (views, likes, comments, shares and follower counts) for Deck District's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.
  • It does not post, comment, message or change anything on any account through these connections.
  • Use of TikTok data through the tool follows TikTok's Terms of Service and Developer Terms.
  • The tool is provided as is. Deck District may change or stop it at any time.
