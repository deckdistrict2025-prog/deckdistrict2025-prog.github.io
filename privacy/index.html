Privacy Policy

The only account connected to Deck District Stats is Deck District's own. The tool does not collect information about anyone else.

  • What is read: public statistics for Deck District's own posts (views, likes, comments, shares) and the account's follower count.
  • What is not read: other people's profiles, messages, contacts or personal details.
  • Where it is kept: in a private repository that only Deck District can access. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Sharing: the data is never sold or shared with anyone.
  • Removal: access can be withdrawn at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Deck District then deletes the stored data.

Questions: contact Deck District through linktr.ee/DeckDistrict.
